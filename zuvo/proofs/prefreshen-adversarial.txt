# Adversarial review (pre-freshen + switch timing)

---

**SEVERITY:** CRITICAL  
**CONFIDENCE:** high  
**FILE:** `src/claude_swap/autoswitch.py` (~1415–1425, `_pre_freshen` loop)  
**ISSUE:** `PREFRESHEN_BUDGET_S` is only checked between loop iterations, not around `_freshen_target`, so one in-flight refresh can exceed the 3s budget by orders of magnitude.  
**ATTACK VECTOR:** In the pre-band, the first candidate that actually hits the network (same class of work the docstring cites at 25–88s) runs to completion inside a “below-threshold” tick. The active slot keeps burning during that wall; you can lose margin before the real switch at `threshold`, which is exactly what pre-freshen was meant to prevent.  
**SUGGESTED FIX:** Enforce a hard cap per call (e.g. remaining budget passed into freshen/HTTP, skip starting a refresh when `remaining <= 0`, or abort/timeout the refresh). Do not rely on a pre-loop check alone.

**SEVERITY:** WARNING  
**CONFIDENCE:** high  
**FILE:** `src/claude_swap/autoswitch.py` (~2529–2533 set `_switch_timing`; ~3095–3105 / ~3115–3120 early returns; ~3134–3136 clear only on success)  
**ISSUE:** `_switch_timing` is set before `_perform_with_drain2` but cleared only on the successful `SwitchEvent` path; `sessions-active`, failed `switch_to`, and similar exits leave it on the engine instance.  
**ATTACK VECTOR:** A later swap that bypasses the freshen loop (`trigger == "consume-first"` or `early`) can still emit `SwitchEvent.timing` merged from stale `freshenMs` / `freshenAttempts` plus fresh `quietScanMs` / `switchMs`. Logs and burn reports attribute slowness to the wrong attempt/candidate and can send tuning in the wrong direction.  
**SUGGESTED FIX:** Clear `_switch_timing` on every `_perform_with_drain2` exit (try/finally), or only attach timing after a confirmed switch; never carry partial state across ticks.
